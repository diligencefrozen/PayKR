Using Apple Pay in Korea

Check your card, ask a cashier in Korean and plan for transit or cash.

Ask the cashier

Show either question to the cashier before paying.

애플페이로 결제할 수 있나요?Can I pay with Apple Pay?

Before you tap

Ask whether the terminal accepts contactless payments and your card network. A contactless sign can remain visible even when the reader is disabled.

Apple · Where you can use Apple Pay

If a payment is declined

Ask the cashier to check the payment method. For a foreign-issued card, contact the issuer about overseas purchases or a declined transaction.

Apple · What to do when a payment is declined

Public transit

Check the transit card required for your journey and its top-up options before boarding. Apple lists the transit systems and cards supported in Wallet.

Apple · Transit systems and cards

Cash and ATMs

For a cash withdrawal, check the ATM’s accepted networks and displayed fee, then confirm overseas withdrawal charges with your card issuer.

Websites and apps

At checkout, look for Apple Pay among the payment options for that order.
